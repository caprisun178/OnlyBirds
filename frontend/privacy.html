Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Only Birds is a bird-watching app built as a student capstone project. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google

  • Your Google account ID, your email address, and the link to your Google profile photo (which we use as your avatar until you set your own).
  • Your Google display name is shown in the app but kept only in your browser, not on our servers.
  • We never see or store your Google password, and we don't request access to anything else in your Google account (no Gmail, Drive, contacts, etc.).

What you add to the app

  • Bird sightings you log: the species, date and time, location (map coordinates), any notes, and any photo you upload.
  • Your settings and lists: your default region, birds you pin, and the notifications the app generates for you.

Problem reports

If you use the "Report a problem" button, we receive your message, the screen and page you were on, your browser's user-agent string, and (if you're signed in) your account ID, by email.

Stored in your browser

Your sign-in session and a short list of locations you've recently used are stored in your browser's local storage. Signing out removes the session; clearing your browser data removes both.

What other people can see

Sightings you log are shared. They appear on the app's public sightings map for everyone, including the species, location, time, any notes, and any photo. Uploaded photos are stored at a public web address. Don't log a sighting at a location you want to keep private (for example, your home).

Your email address is never shown to other users.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your life list, pins, stickers, and settings tied to your account.
  • To show sightings on the map and alert you about birds you've pinned.
  • To look into problems you report.

We don't sell your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

Services we rely on

Running the app means some information passes through these services:

  • Google — sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website.
  • Render — hosts the app's server.
  • Neon — the database storing accounts and sightings.
  • Supabase — stores uploaded photos.
  • eBird, iNaturalist, OpenStreetMap (Nominatim), and Wikipedia/Wikimedia — bird, sighting, place-name, and species information. When you search an area or a place, that location or search text is sent to them to get results.
  • Esri (ArcGIS) — map images, loaded directly by your browser.

Each of these handles data under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

To have your account and everything you've logged deleted, email us from the address you signed in with (below) and we'll remove it. You can also revoke Only Birds' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Only Birds isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
